# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully. Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Be patient with questions and acknowledge mistakes.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or target people with personal attacks.
- Do not post credentials, customer data, or another person's private communications.

This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official project spaces. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request changes, restrict participation, or block repeat offenders. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the behavior involved without exposing private details.

Use GitHub's **Report content** facility for abuse or contact a maintainer through a private channel they explicitly publish. Do not repeat private information in public reports. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
